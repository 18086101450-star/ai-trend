How It Works

AI Trends Hub automatically collects AI-related content from multiple sources, extracts keywords, analyzes trends, and generates predictions. Here's exactly how each part works.

Data Sources

Keywords are extracted from 4 sources on each scan:

  • ArXiv — Latest AI research papers from export.arxiv.org
  • HuggingFace — Trending models and papers on Hugging Face
  • TechCrunch — AI industry news and startup coverage
  • GitHub Trending — Popular AI repositories

Keyword Extraction

Paper titles and abstracts are parsed to extract meaningful AI terms:

  • Single words and bigrams (2-word phrases) are both considered
  • Words shorter than 3 characters or longer than 80 are filtered out
  • Frequent but generic terms are deprioritized via a stopword list
  • Each keyword is classified into a category (Computer Vision, NLP, Robotics, etc.)

Trend Score Formula

Each keyword gets a trend score from 0 to ~1.2, calculated as a weighted combination of 5 factors:

FactorWeightHow It's Calculated
Frequency Score 30% log(1 + recent_count) / log(1 + total_papers) — How often the keyword appears relative to total content
Growth Score 35% min(recent / previous, 3.0) / 3.0 — How fast mentions are growing. New keywords get a base boost of 0.5
Diversity Score 15% min(sources / 3.0, 1.0) — Keywords appearing in more sources score higher
Recency Bonus +0.2 recent / total × 0.2 — Extra boost when recent activity dominates total history
Bigram Bonus +0.2 Two-word phrases (e.g. "reinforcement learning") are more specific and get a fixed bonus
Final Score = Freq × 0.3 + Growth × 0.35 + Diversity × 0.15 + RecencyBonus + BigramBonus

Trending & Emerging

  • Trending — Score > 0.25 and total frequency > 3
  • Emerging — Score > 0.2 and total frequency ≤ 5
  • A keyword can be both trending and emerging simultaneously

Predictions

After each scan, the system analyzes clusters of high-scoring keywords to generate trend predictions:

  • Keywords are grouped by category and correlated by co-occurrence
  • High-growth clusters are identified as potential future trends
  • Each prediction includes a confidence score and estimated time horizon
  • Predictions are for reference only and should not be taken as investment advice

Scan Frequency

  • Scans run on-demand when you click "Scan Now"
  • Each scan takes approximately 60 seconds
  • Results are cumulative — keywords persist across scans
  • Weekly counts track how keyword frequency changes over time
